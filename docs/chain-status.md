## Repair: a perceived block could never become a gather candidate (turn 2, build)

Cause (file:line): the executable gather options have a single source, `TreeView.next` — `wood::gather_candidates` (`src/wood.rs:265-283`) and `catalog::tactical` (`src/catalog.rs:663`); the catalog's own view uses the same source (`src/catalog.rs:106-136`, and `src/catalog.rs:301` rejects the wood target when `verified == 0`). `next` is filled only by the stance search in `wood_tree::inspect` (`src/wood_tree.rs:260-347`), which walks `parents` from `reachable()` (`src/wood_tree.rs:667-713`); every step with a height change requires the ceiling cell at `max(y) + 2` above both columns (`src/wood_tree.rs:700-708`). The adapter's terrain lookup for that map (at the time `src/minecraft.rs:1851-1869`) answered for every non-contact cell exclusively from the current look frame. The ceiling cell above the bot's own column sits 1.88 blocks above eye level, i.e. 75-90 degrees of elevation, while every look sector is a 120-degree cone pointing horizontally or downward and reaching at most 60 degrees above the horizon (`src/perception.rs:42-54`): on a frame-based map, no route with a height change could therefore be verified. At the same time, the planner was stricter than its own validator — `BotNav::verify_route` (`src/minecraft.rs:1357-1381`) reads `standable` and the same ceiling cells at `max(y) + 2` from the loaded world before every step.

Change: one source swap, no new rule. The terrain (`air`, `support`) in the scan lookup now comes from the loaded world (`src/minecraft.rs:1875-1891`), while a cell can still only be a **target** when this observation's frame perceived it (`is_harvestable(name) && (contact || perceived)`); the bot's own contact column keeps its exception. `wood_tree::inspect`, `can_mine`, the line-of-sight and range requirements, the support/upper-log reservation, the memory veto and the `TreeMemory` vote (which still uses the frame, `src/minecraft.rs:1892-1910`) are unchanged, and every step is still authorized by fresh sight of its own swept volume (`observed_wood_step`, `src/minecraft.rs:1503-1545`). `src/catalog.rs` and `src/wood.rs` did not need to change. A perceived block whose break is illegal or unsupported still gets no candidate. `docs/wood-skills.md` describes the cause, the change and the unchanged collection route for drops.

The earlier wording about a missing live rerun has been replaced by the scoped creative trial below. The new terrain reading has run on the server, but its expected `gather_*` effect is not proven. Proof of the overall Survival result requirement — a valid break with the promised drop in the authoritative inventory — is still missing.
